№ 02 — Manifesto

Spell out the position.

This journal stands not on the side that gets used by AI, but on the side that uses AI hard. Around Claude Code, I bundle Stable Diffusion, VOICEVOX, and FFmpeg into one studio, and update — week by week — the ceiling of what one person can ship in a short window.

The tools are new, but the question is the kind that doesn't change for a century. What John Dewey wrote in 1910 and what Claude Code asks of us in 2026 are oddly close. What does it mean to think? What gives a "problem" its outline?

So the Notes, YouTube, GitHub, and X posts here aren't surface-level tool tours. They are the long record of thought needed to use the tools hard. Whether they're useful is for the reader to decide. My job, as the writer, is to leave one question of my own each time.
