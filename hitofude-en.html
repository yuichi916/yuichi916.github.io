Hitofude Hanabi

Draw one line. It becomes a fuse. Fire runs along it, shells burst, and every blast can light another part of your line, so the whole night sky blooms in a chain.

Play now — free

No sign-up, no install. Plays on phones and PCs. The game opens in English.

How to play

  1. Draw one line through the fireworks shells. One stroke, no lifting.
  2. Fire runs along your line and lights the shells it touches.
  3. Chain it all. A burst can reach other parts of your line and keep the chain going. Open every shell for a full bloom.

Your score is points × multiplier. Gold shells and every ten links in a chain raise the multiplier, and a full bloom doubles it. Beat a night's target to earn a charm, and clear eight nights to finish the stage. Stage 1 is the Riverside Festival.
